canvas

Show fans

something new

Engage your fans in a whole new way with Canvas,
a short looping visual you can add to each of your tracks on Spotify.
It’s album artwork, for the streaming age.

Give your music new depth

Canvas appears in the Now Playing view and turns any song into an immersive experience.

Get Noticed

Listeners can easily discover tracks with a Canvas
by swiping through the visual feeds on Home
or tapping into their favorite Spotify playlist.

Share it wide

Your Canvas reaches fans beyond Spotify, too.
When a listener shares your track from the
Spotify app to Instagram Stories, your Canvas will
loop in the background of the Story — along with
a link for fans to 'Play on Spotify'..

Change it up

Add a new Canvas whenever you’re inspired —
whether that’s once for your release or again and
again — to reach new listeners and keep them
coming back.
